# Lab of Labs

A naturalist's field register of frontier science labs: 51 entries (labs, institutes, builders and fleets), each opened as a dossier with its history, research questions, people, outputs, funding, threads to other entries and a hand-placed "position in science" map.

Every fact cites its source; unpinned facts are marked `S?`. Data comes from public lab, institution and news pages, surveyed in rounds (see the survey log on the index).

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is one self-contained page with no build step.
